# Brightline Corp: Company Profile

*Fictional company created for a GRC training exercise.*

## Overview
- **Business:** cloud-based scheduling and workflow software for mid-size organizations
- **Customers:** about 400, including healthcare networks, financial services firms and universities
- **Employees:** about 120 (Phoenix, AZ headquarters; many remote)
- **Founded:** 2018

## Why SOC 2 now
Enterprise customers increasingly require a SOC 2 report before signing or renewing. Brightline has never been audited. The first audit (Type I, Security criteria) is booked for **March 2027**.
